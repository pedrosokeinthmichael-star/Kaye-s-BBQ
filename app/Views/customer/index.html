Why Kaye's

Three things we don't compromise on

01 — The Wood

Post oak, always

No gas, no shortcuts. Every pit runs on post oak logs split fresh, giving the meat its color and its character.

02 — The Time

Twelve-plus hours

Brisket goes on before sunrise. We pull it when it's ready, not when the clock says so.

03 — The Rub

Salt, pepper, patience

Kaye's original bark comes from a simple rub and a lot of restraint — the smoke does the rest of the work.
